How significance is scored

Instagram's data export doesn't include like or comment counts, so every post is scored on what it contains:

  • Carousel depth: more frames usually means a fuller story.
  • Caption length: the posts written about at length.
  • Video, location and milestone language such as exhibitions, films, features, anniversaries and “ten years apart”.

The top twelfth are marked as standouts and shown larger in the timeline.
