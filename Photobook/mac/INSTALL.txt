PHOTOBOOK FOR MAC — HOW TO INSTALL
==================================

Photobook isn't registered with Apple, so macOS refuses a downloaded copy
("Photobook.app is damaged") until it is signed on your own Mac. The
installer does that for you.

1. In this window, right-click "Install Photobook.command" and choose Open.
   (If macOS blocks it: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
   down, click "Open Anyway" next to "Install Photobook.command", and
   confirm.)

2. A Terminal window installs Photobook into Applications and opens it.
   Type your Mac password if it asks (the characters don't show).

3. Eject this disk image. From now on, open Photobook from Applications or
   Launchpad like any other app.

DOING IT BY HAND INSTEAD
------------------------
Drag Photobook onto Applications, then in Terminal run:

    xattr -cr /Applications/Photobook.app
    codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/Photobook.app

WHERE YOUR WORK IS KEPT
-----------------------
Books, photos, templates and fonts are saved on this Mac in
~/Library/Application Support/Photobook. Nothing is uploaded.
Keep a backup: menu (☰) > Download backup, or Back up to a folder.

FONTS
-----
To use the project typefaces (Bobby Jones Soft, TT Norms), open
Book & theme > Fonts > Add font files and choose the .otf files.

UPDATING
--------
Run the installer from the new disk image. Your books stay where they are.

UNINSTALLING
------------
Drag Photobook from Applications to the Trash. To remove your books
too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Photobook.
