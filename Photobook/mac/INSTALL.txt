PHOTOBOOK FOR MAC — HOW TO INSTALL
==================================

1. Drag "Photobook" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Eject the disk image (drag it to the Trash, or right-click > Eject).

3. Open Photobook from Applications or Launchpad.

   The first time, macOS will say it can't check Photobook for malicious
   software, because the app isn't registered with Apple. To allow it:

   - Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down to "Photobook was blocked to protect your Mac" and
     click "Open Anyway". Enter your password if asked, then click
     "Open Anyway" again.

   You only need to do this once. (On macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier you can
   also right-click Photobook in Applications, choose Open, then Open.)

   If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal
   and run this once, then open the app again:

       xattr -cr /Applications/Photobook.app

WHERE YOUR WORK IS KEPT
-----------------------
Books, photos, templates and fonts are saved on this Mac in
~/Library/Application Support/Photobook. Nothing is uploaded.
Keep a backup: menu (☰) > Download backup, or Back up to a folder.

FONTS
-----
To use the project typefaces (Bobby Jones Soft, TT Norms), open
Book & theme > Fonts > Add font files and choose the .otf files.

UPDATING
--------
Drag the new Photobook onto Applications and choose Replace.
Your books stay where they are.

UNINSTALLING
------------
Drag Photobook from Applications to the Trash. To remove your books
too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Photobook.
